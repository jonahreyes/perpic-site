PerPic Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 16, 2026

PerPic is operated by Circles App LLC. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the PerPic mobile application.

Our approach to privacy

PerPic is designed to work primarily on your device. You do not need to create an account to take photos. PerPic does not sell your personal information and does not display third-party advertising inside the app.

If you allow tracking, PerPic uses the Meta Facebook SDK to measure ads that bring people to the app. That measurement is described below. If you choose Ask App Not to Track, PerPic does not turn on that advertiser tracking.

Photos and camera data

PerPic uses your device’s camera when you grant camera permission. Photos captured in PerPic are stored locally on your device.

PerPic accesses your Photos library only when you choose to import an image or export a photo. Exported photos remain in your Photos library until you delete them there.

PerPic does not upload your captured photos or camera sessions to our servers, to RevenueCat, or to Meta.

On-device photo analysis

PerPic may analyze photos on your device to help identify strong images from a session. This processing may evaluate visual characteristics, including sharpness, exposure, composition, and face landmarks.

This analysis runs on your device. PerPic does not use facial recognition to identify you, does not create a biometric identity profile, and does not send your photos or face information to us, to RevenueCat, or to Meta. Analysis results may be stored locally with your session.

Inspiration albums

If you create an inspiration album, the images you select and its album information are stored locally on your device. They are not uploaded by PerPic.

Device motion

PerPic may use device motion information to provide camera leveling guides. This information is processed on your device and is not stored or shared with third parties.

Subscriptions and purchases

PerPic offers optional subscriptions through Apple’s App Store. Apple processes payments and payment credentials. PerPic does not receive your complete payment card information.

PerPic uses RevenueCat to manage subscription status and provide access to paid features. RevenueCat may process purchase history and subscription status for that purpose. PerPic does not send your photos to RevenueCat.

Advertising measurement

If you allow tracking, PerPic shares information with Meta through the Facebook SDK to measure the ads that bring people to the app. That information can include a device identifier, product interaction such as app launches, and purchase history. Meta may link that information with data from other companies’ apps and websites for advertising measurement.

Meta may also receive crash data and other diagnostic data. Those diagnostics are used to operate the SDK. They are not used for tracking.

Photos and camera sessions are not sent to Meta. You can change your choice later in iOS Settings, under Privacy & Security, then Tracking.
